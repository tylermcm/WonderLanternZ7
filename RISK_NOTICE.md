## Narrow experimental scope

This preview targets the **original Nikon Z7, C 3.80, without the paid RAW-video
upgrade**. Do not assume compatibility with a Z7 II, Z6, another Z-series body,
another firmware version, or a body with a different service modification.

The owner reports that the grid feature works and that the latest build retains
3x3 after a shutdown longer than ten seconds on one camera. That is a limited
owner report, not certification or proof that all other functions work. User
presets, settings import/export, wake behavior, extended use and physical
power-failure recovery have not all been validated.

Do not equate a clean checksum with safe firmware. Do not interrupt an update
already running. Preserved stock update files are not a full flash/calibration
backup, and removing the card does not uninstall modified firmware. This project
makes no promise of manufacturer service or warranty coverage.
