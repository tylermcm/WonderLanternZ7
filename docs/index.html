Pixel shift on the original Nikon Z7 — it works*

Experimental SD-card modules for the first-generation Nikon Z7. They add four-shot pixel shift, a shutter sound in Silent photography, upside-down photo playback, custom framing grids and more, by building on Nikon's own firmware services.

Pixel shift on a camera that never had it

Nikon brought pixel shift to newer bodies such as the Z8, Z9 and Zf, but never to the original Z7. This project drives the Z7's in-body stabilisation (IBIS) through Nikon's own BodyVr service. It moves the image sensor exactly one pixel at a time and fires the shutter itself at each position. A computer tool then merges the four raw files into one full-colour image, with no demosaicing.

How a series runs

  1. Half-press to arm. The module waits until stabilisation is active, then for the camera to settle once you let go.
  2. For each of four positions — (0,0), (1,0), (1,1), (0,1) pixels — it moves the sensor, approaching from above, confirms the position from the stabiliser's telemetry, and releases.
  3. It clicks once per shot, recentres the sensor and hands stabilisation back to Nikon.
  4. On a computer, merge_z7_pixel_shift.py measures and combines the four NEFs.

Compared with a single shot of the same scene, the merge resolves fine printed halftone with far less false-colour speckle, and gives smoother, cleaner gradients. The full measurement history, including what didn't work, is in PIXEL_SHIFT_RESEARCH.md.

* What "works" means. It is an experiment, owner-tested on one camera:
  • so far, one clean series with every correction in place;
  • a test build, not yet a menu option;
  • the merge runs on a computer, with no in-camera merge;
  • a faint striping remains from about 0.1 px residual misalignment;
  • it needs a tripod and a still subject, as every pixel-shift camera does.

FAQ

Can the original Nikon Z7 do pixel shift?

Not from Nikon. With this project's experimental modules, yes: four shots one pixel apart through the camera's own IBIS, merged on a computer. The latest series landed within 0.15 px of target. It is still a test build.

Can the Nikon Z7 play a shutter sound in Silent photography?

Yes, with the LCD module. It plays a WAV from the card on each photo, with volume 1–9 and up to ten selectable sounds.

Why does the Z7 show upside-down photos the wrong way up?

The stock firmware saves them as Orientation 1 and playback has no half-turn. The module writes Orientation 3 and rotates them in playback.

Does it work on the Nikon Z7 II?

No. It targets the original Z7 on C 3.80. Z7 II owners should see JKWTCN's z7ii-extensions, the project this port follows.

Can I download a firmware file?

No. The project is source only: no Nikon firmware and no compiled modules are distributed. Read the risk notice before building anything.
